[Dave Nutting Associates (DNA)](https://www.mobygames.com/company/22555/dave-nutting-associates-inc/) made some of the best arcade and console games of the bronze and golden ages, from Gun Fight and Sea Wolf to Gorf and Wizard of Wor. 

[Action Graphics](https://www.mobygames.com/company/1149/action-graphics-inc/) was a direct spinoff, while later staff and founder connections led to [Incredible Technologies](https://amusement.itsgames.com/about/), Ogdon Micro Design and Mammoth Micro Productions, and MacroMind.
